### About

Analyst-engineer who turns the metrics of a 100M+ user marketplace into products people actually use. At Meesho I own DAU and NMV diagnostics for the CXO Office and build the tooling behind them.

### Highlights

- 🤖 **Agentic RCA product on Databricks Genie**: cut a 1–2 day manual RCA to a 5–10 minute report, now run by **100+ analysts, PMs and GMs**. It scored **4.1/5** in blind evaluation, against 3.3 for human analysts and 1.8 for bare Genie.
- 🚨 **Metric Sentinel**: an autonomous daily alerting loop that flags D-1 DAU and Orders/DAU deviations, auto-runs the matching RCA, and posts the root cause to GMs on Slack with an owner and follow-ups.
- 📐 **KPI decomposition**: LMDI, mix/rate and counterfactual attribution behind every waterfall, reconciling to 100% of a period delta.
- 📊 **UG Pulse**: a user-growth app covering 65M+ DAU and ₹200Cr+ GMV/day, with plan-vs-actual tracking, the full funnel, and drill-through into Genie.
- 🏗️ **Certified metric layer**: sole owner of the Platinum metric layer, used by **60+ stakeholders**.
- 📈 **Ranking and catalog analytics**: intent clustering over 21M+ queries (~1.1% NMV), a defect classifier over 12M+ products at 97% accuracy (~1.2% NMV), and catalog filtering over 100M+ SKUs (+16.2% orders/visitor).
- ⚡ **Reporting automation**: cut weekly reporting from 2–3 hours to 2–3 minutes, freeing ~45 hours/week across 41 categories.
