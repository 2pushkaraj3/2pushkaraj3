## 🚀 What I've Built

- 🤖 **Agentic RCA on Databricks Genie**: a YAML config-first engine with deterministic date resolution, column-contract validation and a 24-check reconciliation gate.
- 🚨 **Metric Sentinel**: an autonomous D-1 alerting loop for GMs. It flags DAU and Orders/DAU deviations, auto-runs the matching RCA, and posts an Andon-style ticket to Slack.
- 📐 **KPI decomposition**: LMDI, mix/rate and counterfactual attribution behind every waterfall, reconciling to 100% of a period delta.
- 📈 **UG Pulse**: user-growth analytics covering 65M+ DAU and ₹200Cr+ GMV/day, with plan-vs-actual tracking, the full funnel, and Genie drill-through.
- 🧬 **ML in production**: TF-IDF + SGD supplier-ticket classifier over 430K tickets, and DINOv2 image similarity that replaced a paid API.
