### About

Most of my work comes down to one question: *why did the number move?*

At Meesho I own diagnostics for the marketplace's core health metrics, daily active users and net merchandise value, and I build the tooling that makes answering that question faster and repeatable. Before this I spent 18 months in central analytics working on search ranking, catalog quality and user growth.

### What I work on

- **Automated root-cause analysis**: breaking a metric deviation down across dimension cuts, cohorts and funnel stages to find where the change actually came from
- **Metric anomaly alerting**: daily checks against plan that post an explanation of the movement, not just an alert
- **Self-serve analytics**: dashboards and natural-language query layers on Databricks, so partners in Product, Category and Data Science can answer their own questions
